CASE SUMMARY FOR JURY DELIBERATION SIMULATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Title: People of the State of California v. Orenthal James Simpson
Type: murder
Summary: O.J. Simpson was charged with the murders of his ex-wife Nicole Brown Simpson and Ronald Goldman on June 12, 1994.

Evidence:
1. Blood evidence found at crime scene and defendant's home
2. DNA matches between defendant and crime scene
3. Bloody glove found at defendant's property
4. Footprints matching defendant's shoe size
5. Hair and fiber evidence
6. White Bronco with blood evidence
7. Timeline of defendant's whereabouts
8. History of domestic violence and 911 calls

Prosecution: Physical evidence, DNA matches, and history of domestic violence prove defendant murdered victims in a jealous rage.
Defense: Evidence was contaminated and planted by racist police officers; timeline makes it impossible for defendant to have committed murders.

I do not think, for example, your Honor, that Mr. Goldman can claim a paramount right through some legal artifact for the return of Mr. Simpson's own property. He has been deprived of this property for now going on more than 16 months. It includes personal items of jewelry. It includes photographs and pictures--including pictures of his children. So I do think, your Honor, that there are remedies that are available to the civil litigants in this action.
