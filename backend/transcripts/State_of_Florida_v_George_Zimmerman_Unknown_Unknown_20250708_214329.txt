3. Witness: Jonathan Good saw Martin on top of Zimmerman
4. Forensics: Single gunshot wound at close range

6. Dispatcher told Zimmerman 'we don't need you to' follow

8. No witness saw initial confrontation
9. Martin on phone with Rachel Jeantel during encounter

JURY INSTRUCTIONS:
For guilty verdict, must find depraved mind/ill will (murder 2) or intentional act causing death (manslaughter). Self-defense requires reasonable belief of imminent death/great bodily harm.
